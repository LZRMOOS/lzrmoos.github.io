> Introducing Jimothy: A raccoon with a penchant for notes

a fast, local-first notes & task app with a little too much personality
I've been building a notes and task-list app called Jimothy. It's a side project born out of a simple desire: I wanted something fast, keyboard-first, genuinely fun to open every day, and without AI.

yes, it comes in pink

01 why another notes app

I wanted a notes app that felt like it was built for me specifically, and well, this one is. It's fast to open, easy to access, and endlessly tweakable.

// the_name

Jimothy is a trash panda. He's on the app icon, and if you drop in your own emoji, he can be on your codex icons too.

02 local-first, synced if you want

Every note is just a Markdown file with a YAML frontmatter header. If you want your notes on your phone too, point Jimothy at a Dropbox folder.

today
  • Fully local storage, plain Markdown
  • Dropbox sync desktop <-> mobile
  • Byte-compatible note format
roadmap
  • Google Drive
  • Amazon S3
  • iCloud / WebDAV
